**RealmPacks** is a community-driven collection of **editing packs, thumbnail assets, visual effects, graphics, and other resources** made for RealmCraft creators.

Whether you're building your first channel or creating videos for thousands of viewers, RealmPacks is here to make your workflow easier.

## 🛠️ Installation

Installing a pack is as easy as crafting a wooden pickaxe.

### 1️⃣ Choose your pack

Browse the folders above and find a resource you want to use.

### 2️⃣ Download

Download the pack or individual files from the repository's **Releases** or the relevant folder.

### 3️⃣ Import

Import the files into your editing or design software.

### 4️⃣ Create!

🎥 Start editing.
🖼️ Make your thumbnail.
🚀 Upload your video.
⭐ Repeat.

## 🤝 Contributing

### 🧑‍🌾 Want to add something to the chest?

RealmPacks is built around the creator community, and contributions are welcome!

Before submitting a pack:

* ✅ Make sure you have permission to distribute the assets.
* 📁 Follow the repository's folder structure.
* 📝 Include instructions when necessary.
* 📜 Include licensing/credit information.
* 🧹 Keep files organized and properly named.
* 🔀 Submit a pull request with a clear description.

### 💡 Have an idea?

Open an **Issue** and suggest a new pack, asset, or feature!

## 📜 Credits & Licensing

### ⚠️ Respect the creators.

Not every asset in RealmPacks necessarily has the same license.
